## Quick Resolution Matrix for Users

The following table summarizes common operational situations, their probable causes, and immediate step-by-step solutions:

| Issue or Error Message | Probable Cause | Step-by-Step Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Invalid credentials" upon sign in** | Typing error in password or unregistered email. | 1. Ensure the email has no trailing spaces.<br>2. Click the eye icon to verify typed password.<br>3. If you forgot your password, click **“Forgot your password?”** to reset it via email code. |
| **"Invalid or expired 2FA code"** | Mobile phone time desynchronization or expired 30s code. | 1. Wait for your authenticator app to generate a fresh 6-digit code.<br>2. Ensure your mobile phone's clock is set to automatic network time.<br>3. Use an emergency recovery code if mobile app access is unavailable. |
| **Dashboard or Portfolio appears empty** | Active restrictive filter or no projects registered yet. | 1. Click the **[Clear Filters]** button in the toolbar.<br>2. Check if the "My projects" tab is active without assigned works; switch to "All".<br>3. Wait a few moments or refresh your browser if the platform was just opened. |
| **[Create Project] or [Add Specialist] buttons missing** | Your account does not have administrative permissions. | 1. Check your role in the upper right profile widget.<br>2. Standard Technical User accounts have view permissions and capability to update their own assigned tasks. |
| **No alerts received by email or Discord** | Make Webhook link disconnected or scenario turned off in Make.com. | 1. Navigate to **Integrations** (`Shift + 7`).<br>2. Check that the Make card displays **Connected**.<br>3. Click **[Test Connection]** to diagnose the link.<br>4. Confirm on Make.com that the scenario is active (**ON**). |
| **Error when downloading system backup** | Incorrect confirmation password. | 1. Confirm you are entering the password of the active Administrator account.<br>2. Ensure your account holds primary Administrator privileges. |

## Support and Assistance Channels

If an issue persists after following the resolution matrix:
- **Internal Support:** Contact the PMO or IT Systems coordination via corporate email.
- **Audit Verification:** If you observe discrepancies in timeline calculations or schedule slippage, export the CSV report from **Project Audit** (`Shift + 9`) and attach it to your support request.
